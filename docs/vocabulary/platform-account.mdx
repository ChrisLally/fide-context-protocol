## Summary

- **Definition:** An authority-based account principal (e.g., an email address or a specific GitHub, Google, or Mastodon user ID). The identity exists by the permission of a host system's database.
- **Not:** Authenticated by a host authority's database, not purely by mathematics.
- **Layer:** [Network Anchors](/docs/fcp/vocabulary#network-anchors)
- **Hex Code:** `21`
- **Standard Alignment:** `schema:OnlineAccount` (Close fit)
