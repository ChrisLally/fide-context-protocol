## Summary

- **Definition:** A structured collective of people or agents that acts as a unit.
- **Not:** Not a single person or software. Not a query-defined group (e.g. alumni by year); create only when the group itself needs to act.
- **Layer:** [Agents](/docs/fcp/vocabulary#agents)
- **Hex Code:** `11`
- **Standard Alignment:** `org:Organization` (Exact fit)
