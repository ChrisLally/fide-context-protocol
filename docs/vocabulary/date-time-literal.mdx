## Summary

- **Definition:** A specific point in time (ISO 8601 formatted, UTC normalized).
- **Not:** Not a date or time alone.
- **Layer:** [Literals](/docs/fcp/vocabulary#literals)
- **Hex Code:** `a6`
- **Standard Alignment:** `rdf:Literal` + `xsd:dateTime` (Exact fit)
