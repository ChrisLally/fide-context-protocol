## Summary

- **Definition:** A calculated amount of elapsed time (ISO 8601 duration format).
- **Not:** Not a datetime.
- **Layer:** [Literals](/docs/fcp/vocabulary#literals)
- **Hex Code:** `a7`
- **Standard Alignment:** `rdf:Literal` + `xsd:duration` (Exact fit)
