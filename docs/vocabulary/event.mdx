## Summary

- **Definition:** An occurrence or happening bounded in time. Can be observed/aggregated; may be caused by Actions, systems, or nature. Occurs at a Place (physical or virtual).
- **Not:** Not a specific exertion of agency (Action).
- **Layer:** [Spacetime](/docs/fcp/vocabulary#spacetime)
- **Hex Code:** `41`
- **Standard Alignment:** `schema:Event` (Exact fit)
