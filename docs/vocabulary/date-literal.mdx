## Summary

- **Definition:** A calendar date (YYYY-MM-DD formatted according to ISO 8601).
- **Not:** Not a datetime or time.
- **Layer:** [Literals](/docs/fcp/vocabulary#literals)
- **Hex Code:** `a4`
- **Standard Alignment:** `rdf:Literal` + `xsd:date` (Exact fit)
