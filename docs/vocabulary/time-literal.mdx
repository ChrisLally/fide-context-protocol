## Summary

- **Definition:** A time of day (formatted according to ISO 8601, represented in UTC).
- **Not:** Not a date or datetime.
- **Layer:** [Literals](/docs/fcp/vocabulary#literals)
- **Hex Code:** `a5`
- **Standard Alignment:** `rdf:Literal` + `xsd:time` (Exact fit)
